About

Our history

Founded in 2010 at UCLA, Sigma Eta Pi is a co-ed professional fraternity dedicated not to setting a path for you, but to supporting you on whatever path you carve. The UNC chapter, the Iota chapter of Sigma Eta Pi, was established in Spring 2020.

Our mission

To cultivate the next generation of entrepreneurs by building a strong community of students passionate about starting ventures, solving real-world problems, and making a lasting impact.

Our values

What we hold ourselves to.

Integrity

We hold ourselves to high standards, in ventures, in class, and with each other.

Creativity

New ideas are the whole point. We make space for the unconventional ones.

Collaboration

Co-ed, cross-major, cross-class. The best things we build, we build together.

Grit

Startups are hard. We push one another to keep going and grow from it.
