Recruitment FAQ

Who can rush SEP?

Any UNC undergraduate, any major, any year. We're co-ed and interest in entrepreneurship matters more than experience.

Do I need a startup or business background?

No. Members come from CS, business, design, pre-med, journalism and more. Curiosity and drive are what we look for.

When does recruitment happen?

At the start of each semester. Follow our Instagram and join the recruitment GroupMe for dates the moment they drop.

What's the time commitment?

Weekly chapter meetings plus events you opt into: workshops, socials, and venture work. Most members balance it easily with coursework.
